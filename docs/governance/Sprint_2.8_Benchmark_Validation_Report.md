# Sprint 2.8 — Benchmark Validation Report

## 1. Objective

Validates that locked benchmark golden datasets can be executed against target AI configurations to produce immutable results.

---

## 2. Validation Scope

- **Dataset Locking:** Ensures once locked, benchmark items cannot be mutated.
- **Run Execution:** AI evaluations are triggered, latency and cost logged.
- **Agreement Analysis:** Automated comparison vs. gold-standard human scores.
