# ADR 024: Domain-Driven Design (DDD) in Exam Product Domain

## Context

The examination management structure of Clasptek is core business logic. The original implementation used flat skills, legacy exam sections, and direct active-record structures. This spread validation rules across routes and persistence files, creating invariant validation drift.

## Decision

We establish a pure Domain-Driven Design model using:

- **Aggregates:** Explicit boundaries of consistency like `ExamProduct`, `OfficialExamStructure`, `AssessmentBlueprint`, `SkillFramework`, `LearningFramework`, `ReadinessFramework`, and `DiagnosticFramework`.
- **Value Objects:** Validate boundary inputs and hold domain constraints (e.g. `ExamCode`, `VersionNumber`, `ScoreBand`).
- **Specifications:** Decouple boolean business logic from aggregates and services (e.g. `CanPublishExamProduct`, `BlueprintIsComplete`).

## Consequences

- Business invariants are transactionally guaranteed.
- Reusable validation logic across bounded contexts.
- Decouples persistence from domain models.
